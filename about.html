About Us

More than clothing-this is a movement of faith, purpose and identity in Christ.

Jesus Gang Apparel was created with a vision to spread the message of Jesus Christ through fashion.What started as a simple idea has grown into a brand that inspires believers to boldly express their faith.

Meet The Founder

Kago Nyama

The founder of Jesus Gang Apparel is passionate about combining faith and creativity.With a heart for God and a vision to inspire others, this brand was created to help people boldly represent Christ in their everyday lives.This brand is not all about clothing, it's about spreading the gospel.

Our Mission

To create a meaningful christian clothing that encourages people to stand firm in their faith, represent Christ boldly and inspire others through fashion.

Our Vision

To build a global community of believers who use fashion as a tool to spread hope, faith and the love of Christ.

Our Values

  • Faith:Everything we do centered on Christ.
  • Quality:We deliver comfortable and lasting products.
  • Boldness:We encourage confidence in faith.
